Local Transcriber

Transcribe audio and video files entirely on your device — no uploads, no cloud.

  • Private by design: your files never leave your device.
  • Models are downloaded on first use — the app automatically picks the best fit for your device. More powerful devices download larger, more accurate models (up to ~5 GB total); less powerful devices get smaller, faster ones (~500 MB). A WiFi connection is recommended.
  • Performance depends on your device: the app will automatically try lighter models if needed, but older or low-memory devices may have limitations.
  • AI models are not perfect: transcriptions and extracted events may contain mistakes. Larger models tend to be more accurate, but require more resources and can still make errors.
